# TrainPilot phone 1.2.14 /2721 — Wear Quick Workout integration (candidate)

The phone's existing canonical **Gyors edzés** / Quick Workout workflow is now available from the paired Wear app. The watch sends validated `startQuickWorkout` and `addQuickExercise` commands, using the exercise IDs from the phone's synced exercise library (including custom exercises). The phone creates a real `type: 'quick'`, `quickWorkout: true` session without inventing a program.

- A watch-created quick session uses the same immutable workout ID as the phone session, even across replay, offline completion and retries.
- Completed sets/reps/load and measured Health Services results attach to the **same** Journal workout; late measured totals update the existing row without creating duplicates.
- A stale start cannot replace an unrelated active workout or unfinished phone draft. A missing/unknown exercise ID is rejected.
- The phone shares a bounded exercise picker catalog with the watch. The watch still uses the independent Health Services recording.
- No change to Samsung Health data collection, existing phone Quick Workout actions, package ID or signing key.

## Required validation
- Phone Node regressions including Wear quick lifecycle; existing full 83-script Chromium suite; Android/JVM tests; performance budgets; signed phone APK and signer continuity.
- Real phone + Galaxy Watch validation of start, add exercise, finish, replay and **one** saved quick entry containing wearable metrics.
- VersionName **1.2.14** / versionCode **2721**. This is a candidate; do not promote to main or publish an APK before physical acceptance.

## 2026-10-09: Wear vs Health Connect workout measurements (#158)

A completed workout may contain two genuinely different measurements:
- The `healthWear129` record is the **TrainPilot Wear OS Health Services exercise session** for the matching `workoutId`. If validated and present, the phone Journal's Health panel now highlights it by default.
- The `health240` record is the **Health Connect / selected Samsung Health-provider training-window** result. It may contain daily/window calories unrelated to the exact live watch exercise session.

The journal allows explicit source switching **only when matching Wear metrics exist**. Its source values are never combined or added. Missing data, a zero, and a partial session remain distinct. Existing historical workouts without attached Wear measurements continue showing their Health Connect data; a past screenshot alone does not establish a recoverable Wear summary. The canonical phone Journal remains the source of truth for workout records.

This candidate also publishes a small, read-only mini-Journal projection (up to eight finished workouts) to Wear, excluding pictures and sensitive whole-day Health ledgers.
