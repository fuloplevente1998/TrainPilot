# TrainPilot 1.3.1 /2727 — Phone-started watch measurement (candidate)

Both phone and Wear use **1.3.1 /2727**, with their existing application IDs and signing identity.

Starting a new program or quick workout on the phone first publishes the active workout, then asks the connected watch to open TrainPilot through AndroidX RemoteActivityHelper. The watch briefly wakes its screen and starts the existing foreground Health Services recording only after receiving the matching workout ID and published revision. The screen can then sleep while recording continues.

This addresses the confirmed #157 trigger: starting on the phone while leaving the watch untouched previously only saved the snapshot; opening the watch Activity was required to start measurement. A successful remote-open request is not proof of sensor recording.

- Existing watch measurement preference and sensor permissions remain required. Disabled measurement stays disabled. Other apps' exercise recordings are not interrupted.
- Routine polling, restored drafts, returning to the phone, canceled starts and watch-originated commands do not remotely reopen the watch. Pending starts expire after 15 seconds or when the phone leaves the foreground or the workout changes.
- A failed remote launch preserves the phone workout and explains that TrainPilot can be opened manually on the watch. More than one connected watch is rejected to avoid duplicate recording.
- Mini Journal, measurement history, source separation and already logged health values remain intact. The earlier Wear 4 kcal versus phone 1 active / 2 total kcal report remains open for exact window/provider comparison.

Validation: full phone runtime handoff coverage and existing Node suite; watch identity/revision guard tests; paired signed phone/Wear release gates and six Chromium groups.
