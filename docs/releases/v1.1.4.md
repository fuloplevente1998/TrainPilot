# TrainPilot 1.1.4 /2702 — #110 phase 1 candidate

The 12-second watch scan becomes a one-minute interruptible scan with countdown. Up to 120 retained results use a bounded priority policy so a late selected/watch candidate can replace an ordinary neighbour. Retained row numbers stay stable.

A supported connected watch can be explicitly remembered in private native preferences. Reopening/resuming the trial reconnects to the saved address, checks its service and limits transient failure retries to three attempts. Disconnect and Forget remain usable during connection; forget/local erase remove the saved selection. Device addresses are excluded from exports/backups/cloud.

Includes previous 2698–2701 candidate features and the verified battery/current-step trial. Battery and a valid zero step count were physically confirmed on 2701. New scan/reconnect behavior needs phone acceptance. This phase does not yet implement historical health import, the unified Health Connect/BLE native store or background sync; see [the roadmap](../HEALTH_JOURNAL_ROADMAP.md) and [phone steps](../BLUETOOTH_GT4PRO.md).

Validation evidence and the signed upgrade-safe APK are attached to the phase PR after all gates pass. Stable main and the published 2701 candidate remain unchanged.
